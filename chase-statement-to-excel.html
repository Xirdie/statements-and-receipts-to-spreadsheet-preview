      Questions

      Is it free?

      The preview is: text PDFs are read in your browser and checked against the balance at no cost, and scanned pages are counted and priced. The download is $9 per upload (up to 60 scanned pages), $19 a month for 150 pages, or $29 for 250 pages that never expire.

      What if the numbers don't add up?

      The page that breaks the chain is re-read once by a stronger model at no charge. If it still doesn't add up, the rows are flagged in the file and you can ask for your money back.

      Which pages count toward my limit?

      Scanned pages and receipt images. Text PDF pages don't: your browser reads them, and if one doesn't add up its text (never an image) is read by the AI at no page cost, up to 200 such pages per job.

      What does "unreconciled" mean?

      Card statements and receipts have no running balance to check against. We check what can be checked (summary totals, subtotal + tax + tip) and label the rest honestly.

      What happens to my statements?

      They stay in your browser tab. Scanned pages, and text pages that don't add up, are sent to Anthropic's AI to be read after you pay; Anthropic keeps API data for up to 30 days and then deletes it. This site keeps no copy. Full detail on the Privacy page.

      Can I use the file for my taxes?

      It is a conversion, not tax or accounting advice. Check every figure against your statement before you rely on it. Receipt categories are suggestions. Statements from any bank with dates, descriptions and amounts work; US, UK, Indian and European number formats are read.
