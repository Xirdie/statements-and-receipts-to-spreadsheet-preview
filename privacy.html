Privacy

Your files

  • Text PDFs are read in your browser. When their rows add up, they never leave your device.
  • Scanned pages, receipt photos, and the text of pages that don't add up are sent after you pay, a few pages at a time, through our server to Anthropic (Claude), which reads the rows. Our server passes each page on and keeps no copy.
  • Anthropic keeps API data for up to 30 days, then deletes it (Anthropic's data retention).
  • Your files, page images and spreadsheet stay in this tab's memory. We clear your files and page images when you download, and everything when you close the tab.

Payments and purchase records

  • Stripe handles payment. We see your email and your order (the checkout record), never your card. Your purchase code is kept in this browser so you can use remaining pages; 'Forget my purchases' removes it.
  • We keep each job's page counts, a fingerprint (hash) of the files (never their content), which pages were read and the purchase id for 30 days (one-off) or while your pack or plan lasts, so pages can't be used twice. For packs and plans that record is filed under your Stripe customer id.

Our server, analytics and bug reports

  • A keyed hash of your IP address, kept 48 hours, limits bug reports; a short-lived per-address rate limit on our server protects it from floods.
  • Our server, the anti-bot check and our cookieless analytics run on Cloudflare, which sees your IP address and browser details as any host does.
  • Bug reports (what happened, the page, your browser, your purchase code if any, and your email if you give it) go to our private records; the email is used only to reply.
  • A bug report waits on our server until our weekly import, at most about a week, then moves to our private records, which are kept and not deleted on a schedule.
